## Why I saved this

A short, practical list of free design references for the next web project, saved from an X post by Viktor Oddy (@viktoroddy, posted Oct 29, 2026).

## Notes

The seven resources, as listed in the post:

- [motionsites.ai](https://motionsites.ai) — free premium website prompts (backgrounds, motion sections) meant to be pasted into Claude
- [land-book.com](https://land-book.com) — landing page inspiration
- [recent.design](https://recent.design) — web design inspiration gallery
- [landdding.com](https://landdding.com) — landing page gallery
- [fontinlogo.com](https://fontinlogo.com) — the fonts behind well-known brands
- [godly.website](https://godly.website) — award-winning websites
- [60fps.design](https://60fps.design) — mobile UI animation references

The post also carried a short video collage of website and animation examples.
