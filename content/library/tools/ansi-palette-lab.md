A terminal truecolor / 256-color lab.

It features an interactive xterm 256-color cube/grid, an RGB-to-nearest-xterm256 converter to show quantization error side-by-side with truecolor original, and a theme builder where you can assign the standard 16 ANSI colors, plus foreground/background/cursor, and preview them in a realistic mock terminal pane.
