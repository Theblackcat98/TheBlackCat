A collection of browser-based online tools.

[Open the live tools](https://theblackcat98.github.io/Online-Tools/) — hash and checksum calculators, Base32/Base58/Base64, HTML and URL encoding, and image converters, plus two larger apps:

- [Color Token Workshop](https://theblackcat98.github.io/Online-Tools/color-token-workshop.html) — OKLCH palette builder with WCAG 2.1 and APCA contrast checks and CSS/JSON export.
- [Mermaid Viewer](https://theblackcat98.github.io/Online-Tools/mermaid-viewer.html) — render Mermaid diagrams with pan and zoom, and export SVG or PNG.
