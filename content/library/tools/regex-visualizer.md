## What it is

The Regex Visualizer is a client-side tool to test JavaScript regular expressions against target text. It highlights full matches in context and breaks out capture groups into a readable table.

## Why it's useful

Regular expressions are notoriously difficult to read and debug. Most online tools require sending your test data to a server.

- **Live evaluation:** As you type the regex, the highlights and capture groups update immediately.
- **Privacy:** Entirely client-side. Safe for testing regexes against logs or sensitive context.
- **Group extraction:** Clearly separates capture groups (`$1`, `$2`, etc.) in the results table so you know exactly what your regex is extracting.
