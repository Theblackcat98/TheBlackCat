## Why I saved this

A field guide to free AI access that stresses checking the fine print — whether an offer is free forever or expiring credit, whether it's hobby-grade or production-safe, and what it costs in data. Saved from an X Article by MIKE (@mikenevermiss, posted Aug 19, 2026).

## Notes

No-card free API tiers covered, each with its stated limits and catches:

- Google AI Studio (Gemini Flash / Flash-Lite and 2.5 Pro)
- Groq
- Cerebras
- Mistral La Plateforme (Experiment tier)
- Cloudflare Workers AI (allowance measured in Neurons, not tokens)
- OpenRouter (free models; a one-time credit purchase raises the daily cap)
- Cohere (trial key, non-commercial)
- OpenAI (no permanent free tier; free usage via its data-sharing program)

Also covered:

- Trial credits and why generic cloud trial credits may not pay for Gemini API use
- Startup/funding programs (including a correction on where the widely quoted Anthropic credit figure comes from)
- Students: the GitHub Student Developer Pack and Copilot Student plan, plus SheerID-verified offers
- Open-source maintainers: Claude for Open Source, OpenAI Codex for Open Source, and GitHub Copilot for maintainers
- Regional availability limits
- Local/self-hosted models (llama.cpp, Ollama, MLX, ROCm on AMD) framed as the only tier that can't be revoked, with routing/backoff patterns for when hosted free tiers rate-limit
