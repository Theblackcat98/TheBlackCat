## What it is

The OpenGraph Previewer allows you to input OpenGraph meta tag values (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`) and instantly see how different platforms will render them as a rich link card.

## Why it's useful

Every platform truncates text differently and applies different CSS styles to social cards. Finding out that your `og:image` is cropped badly after you publish is frustrating.

- **Multi-platform:** Simulates the rendering logic of X/Twitter (Summary with Large Image) and LinkedIn simultaneously.
- **Text truncation testing:** Visually see where long titles or descriptions get cut off (`...`) by CSS line-clamp.
- **Client-side:** Perfect for testing local assets or drafts before deploying them.
