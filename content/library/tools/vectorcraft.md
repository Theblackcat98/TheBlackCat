## Why I saved this

Part of the ArtCraft suite of pure-Rust Adobe clones — the Illustrator equivalent, agent-drivable over MCP.

## Notes

- Vector illustration; clean-room reimplementation of **Adobe Illustrator** (~2.1k stars, Oct 2026).
- Native on macOS, Windows, Linux and FreeBSD; in the browser via WebAssembly.
- Ships an MCP server for agents (README badge).
- Pure Rust. Dual-licensed MIT / Apache-2.0. Status: in active development.
- Siblings: PhotoCraft, LightCraft, FilmCraft, EffectCraft, DesignCraft, PdfCraft.
