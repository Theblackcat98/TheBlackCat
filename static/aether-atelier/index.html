II. Phyllotaxis & Harmonographs

Botanical primordia pack most efficiently when successive elements diverge by the Golden Angle $\phi = 360^\circ(1 - 1/\varphi) \approx 137.50776^\circ$. Deviating by even $0.1^\circ$ transforms Fibonacci spirals into radial spokes. Harmonographs model damped coupled pendulums.

III. Cymatics & Morphogenesis

Alan Turing's 1952 Reaction-Diffusion model describes how two chemical morphogens reacting and diffusing at unequal rates spontaneously self-organize into spots, stripes, and coral labyrinths. Ernst Chladni's equation maps acoustic standing-wave nodal lines.
