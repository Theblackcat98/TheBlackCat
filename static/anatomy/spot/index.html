How Spot works

Boston Dynamics' Spot on a gait bench: a 1,100 × 500 × 840 mm quadruped, 40.5 kg with the Spot Arm fitted — 12 leg actuators plus the arm's 6 degrees of freedom, a swappable 605 Wh battery and a 1.6 m/s top speed. The arm reaches 984 mm and its gripper opens to 175 mm.
