## Why I saved this

Part of the ArtCraft suite of pure-Rust Adobe clones — this one takes on Premiere Pro, the video-editing pillar.

## Notes

- Video editing, color and sound; clean-room reimplementation of **Adobe Premiere Pro** (~3.1k stars, Oct 2026).
- Clean-room take on the Premiere workflow, by the ArtCraft team.
- Native on macOS, Windows and Linux; in the browser via WebAssembly.
- Pure Rust. Dual-licensed MIT / Apache-2.0. Status: "young and moving fast".
- Siblings: PhotoCraft, LightCraft, VectorCraft, EffectCraft, DesignCraft, PdfCraft.
