## Why I saved this

"Make this faster" vs "find the slowest page, improve it, measure, repeat" — a well-articulated pattern library for the "improve until X" jobs I keep running. The catalog is browsable without installing anything.

## Notes

Two parts: the Loop Library website ([signals.forwardfuture.com/loop-library](https://signals.forwardfuture.com/loop-library/)) and the optional `loopy` skill (discover · find · audit · repair · craft · run · debrief · save). Non-Loopy agents can consume the catalog via `llms.txt`, `catalog.json` or `catalog.txt`. Each loop states its goal, check, next step and stop condition.
