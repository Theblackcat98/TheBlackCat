## What it is

An earlier reconstruction of the same subject, kept alongside the finished piece for comparison. Same drafting-sheet language, same axonometric camera, less of the structure resolved.

## What differs from the finished piece

- The terraces carry planting, but as scattered greenery rather than continuous hedged bands, so the terraces read as ledges with shrubs on them.
- Water falls down the tiers, but as single sheets without the basins, ripple rings, mist and splash of the later version.
- The mass is drawn to be viewed from the front: some faces are thinner than the others, and the site around it is suggested rather than built.
- The later piece adds the closure work — a solid core per tier, backed arch openings, planting on all four sides — so it survives a full orbit. This one does not.

## Why it is here

Because the difference between the two is the whole point of the exercise: the first pass is a picture of the Hanging Gardens, the second is a model of them. Both are one self-contained HTML file with no dependencies.
